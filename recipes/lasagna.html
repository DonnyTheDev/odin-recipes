Lasagna Recipe

How to make the best Lasagna in the world

Ingredients:

  • Meat
  • Onion and garlic
  • Tomato products
  • Sugar
  • Spices and seasonings
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Cheeses
  • Eggs

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, brown the meat and drain any excess fat.
  4. Add the onion and garlic, and cook until softened.
  5. Stir in the tomato products, sugar, and spices.
  6. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  7. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, and cheeses in a baking dish.
  8. Bake for 30 minutes, or until bubbly and golden brown.
